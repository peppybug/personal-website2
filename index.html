Camp III · 7,162 m

Can free products win a market?

School research · Economics · Sint Vitus College, 2026

To what extent is offering free products an effective strategy for tech companies to gain market share?

“In hoeverre is het aanbieden van gratis producten een effectieve strategie voor Tech bedrijven om marktaandeel te winnen?”

Interviews

  • Relations ManagerMeta
  • Principal Growth ManagerGoogle
  • Shane GreensteinProfessor, Harvard Business School
Method
Each expert answered the same set of questions in a one-on-one interview, and I compared their answers side by side.
Findings
Free products win market share fast, but they only pay off when the company has a clear way to earn money later, through ads, data or paid upgrades.
Outcome
Graded 10/10.
