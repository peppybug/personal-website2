# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Anyone who comes across Olivier B. Janssen: people curious about him, from the gaming and creator community to people in film, tech and games. There is no single target audience; the site is a personal showcase for whoever opens the link.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio for Olivier B. Janssen that presents his work across games, tech, film and TV as a climb up Mount Everest, from Base Camp (5,364 m) to the summit (8,849 m). Success is that a visitor is impressed and remembers him; there is no conversion goal.

## Positioning
Not a résumé page: the visitor makes the ascent themselves. Scrolling is climbing, each section is a camp on the south route, and the reward at the end is reaching the summit.

## Operating Context
Shared as a link (GitHub Pages: https://peppybug.github.io/personal-website2/), opened on phones and desktops. A custom domain is planned but not yet bought.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS; no build framework. `npm run build` copies public files to `_site/` and checks local links. Deployed with GitHub Actions to GitHub Pages.
- The climb concept is binding: Base Camp → Camp I → Camp II → Camp III → Camp IV → Summit, with altitudes 5,364 / 6,065 / 6,400 / 7,162 / 7,950 / 8,849 m and a scroll-driven altitude readout.
- Illustrations must be original (inspired by the mood of the game Cairn, never its art, characters or logo).
- Motion must respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Nothing is pushed to `main` until Olivier says so.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Olivier B. Janssen. Tagline: "I make things come to life."
- Voice: bold and adventurous, an expedition-journal tone.
- Summit headline: "You made it to the top." followed by "Thanks for climbing all the way up."
- Palette heritage: Onyx Black #01010D, Electric Indigo #6331FB, Tropical Mint #88F9C0 (used as accents in the current climb).

## Product Principles
1. The climb is the experience: every section earns its place as a step up the mountain.
2. Impress, then get out of the way: spectacle frames the facts, never buries them.
3. Truth over hype: only real roles, credits and projects.
4. Bold voice, short words: say it like an expedition log, not a CV.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable text over every scene, keyboard-reachable navigation, and a complete, still version for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
